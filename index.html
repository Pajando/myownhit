    How it works

    No calls unless you want one.

    1. You tell us the story

      Answer a few questions on the site, about five minutes, or reply to our email. You can even send a voice memo telling the story.

    2. We confirm the details

      Within 2–3 days, Alejandro emails you to confirm everything and how to pay. Nothing gets made until you confirm.

    3. We write and produce it

      Lyrics from your details, produced in the style you picked, checked line by line by a person.

    4. You get the song

      Usually 2–5 days after you confirm. A 3–4 minute song by email, yours to keep, play at the party, and share.

    Finally, your own song.

    This is your chance. Not a song you like. A song about you, yours for life. Enter your email and we'll take you to the questions. About five minutes.

    Questions

    How much does a song cost?

    See pricing. Each song is 3–4 minutes. If you want more than 3 songs, email us and we'll put together a price.

    How is the song made?

    The lyrics are written from the details you send. The track is produced with modern AI music tools, then shaped, reviewed, and finished by a person. You get a finished song, not a template with a name dropped in.

    How long does it take?

    About 4–8 days total. We confirm the details within 2–3 days, and once you confirm, most songs are ready in 2–5 days. If you need it by a certain date, put it in the form and we'll tell you if it's tight.

    Can it be in Spanish?

    Yes. Spanish, English, or both in one song. Written in the language you choose by someone who speaks it, not translated by software.

    What do I get?

    A finished audio track by email. Yours to keep, play at the event, and share with the person it's about. If it's for business use, like an ad or a jingle, check that box in the form.

    Can I hear an example first?

    Yes. Check "Send me an example song in my style" in the form, and you'll get one before you decide.
