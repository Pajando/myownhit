What is this?

The My Own Hit Studio is a small 3D walk-through of how a custom song gets made. The stops:

  1. Tell your story: Answer a few questions on the site, about five minutes, or reply to our email. You can even send a voice memo telling the story. No calls unless you want one.
  2. Pick your style: In the style you've always wanted.
  3. English, Spanish, or both: Spanish, English, or both in one song. Written in the language you choose by someone who speaks it, not translated by software.
  4. You, or someone you love: A song about you, or one for someone else: a wedding, a quinceañera, a birthday, a tribute. A song for the whole family works too, and so does one for your business.
  5. How your song is made: Your lyrics are written from what you tell us. The music is produced with modern AI tools, then a real person shapes it, checks every line, and finishes it.
  6. What it costs: Pay right after you send your story. If we can't make your song, you get a full refund. See pricing →
  7. Hear demos first: Not ready yet? Tap your style on the site and hear songs made the same way yours will be. Listen →
  8. Start my song: It takes about five minutes. Not a song you like. A song about you, yours for life. Start my song →
