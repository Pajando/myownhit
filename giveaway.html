Free song giveaway

Three people win a personal song for their ride. Here are the rules, in plain words.

Official rules · Last updated: October 18, 2026

No purchase necessary

You don't have to buy anything to enter or win. Buying a song does not improve your chances.

Who can enter

Legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older when they enter. Void where prohibited. People who work for My Own Hit or Ojeda Works, and their immediate families, can't win.

When

The giveaway starts when the giveaway post goes live on Instagram and ends Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 11:59 PM Pacific Time. Entries after that don't count.

How to enter

  1. Follow the Instagram account that posted the giveaway.
  2. Comment AO on the giveaway post.
  3. Share the post, for example to your Story or with a friend.

One entry per person, no matter how many times you comment. Instagram doesn't let us see every share, so we check the follow and the comment. Entries using fake or multiple accounts don't count.

How winners are picked

On Sunday, October 25, 2026, we pick 3 winners at random from all eligible entries. Your odds depend on how many people enter.

How winners are told

We message winners by direct message on Instagram from the account that posted the giveaway. Reply within 72 hours, or we pick another winner at random. We will never ask you for money, a card number, or a password. If someone does, it isn't us.

The prize

Each winner gets one personal song (3 to 4 minutes), written and produced by My Own Hit about the story the winner shares with us. Approximate retail value: $47 each, $141 for all three prizes. No cash instead of the song, and the prize can't be transferred. We'll deliver the song within 30 days after we get your story details.

Your information

We only use what winners share with us to run the giveaway and make their song, as described in our privacy page. We won't post a winner's song or story unless the winner says yes in writing first. Winners pay any taxes on their prize.

Instagram

This giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by, or associated with, Instagram or Meta. By entering, you release Instagram and Meta from any responsibility for it.

Who runs it
